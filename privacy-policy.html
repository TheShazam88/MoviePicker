Movie Night: Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · applies to the Movie Night app for Android (io.github.theshazam88.movienight)
In short: Movie Night has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The developer never receives any of your data.

What the app stores, and where

Your household, its profiles, PINs, ratings, watchlists, watch history, added titles, settings, and any TMDB or OMDb keys you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Android's cloud backup is switched off for this app. If you move to a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer, your household can be copied directly to the new phone. The in-app Backup & restore saves a file to a place you choose, and copies the backup to your clipboard. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

When the app connects to the internet

The app works offline. It only goes online when you use one of these optional features:

  • Update from TMDB: with a TMDB key you enter yourself, the app downloads lists of popular and new films and series from The Movie Database (api.themoviedb.org). The request contains your key; it contains nothing about you or what you have watched.
  • Critic scores: with an OMDb key you enter yourself, the app asks the OMDb API (www.omdbapi.com) for scores, one title at a time. The request contains your key and the title and year being looked up. When you choose "for what you have watched", those are titles you have watched.
  • Where to watch and Get a free key links open JustWatch, TMDB or OMDb in your browser.

These services receive your IP address like any website you visit, and handle it under their own privacy policies: TMDB, OMDb, JustWatch. The developer receives nothing from them.

Permissions

The app asks for internet access only, which the optional features above need. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. Saving a backup goes through Android's own Save dialog, so the app only touches the file you pick.

Children

Profiles can be set up for children by an adult in the household, with a content level that limits which titles they see. The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Use Delete this household in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. That deletes everything, because nothing is kept anywhere else.
